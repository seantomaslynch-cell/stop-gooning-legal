Privacy Policy

Stop Gooning
Operated by Sean Lynch
Effective date: October 26, 2026
Last updated 2026-10-24

Stop Gooning is a quit streak tracker. It is built to keep your data on your phone. There is no account, no email and no sign in. This policy explains the small amount of information that leaves your device, who gets it and why.

1. What stays on your device

These are stored only on your phone and we cannot see them:

  • Your current streak, longest streak and reset history.
  • The reason tag you pick when you log a reset.
  • Your daily check-ins (mood, urge level and any note you type).
  • Your quiz answers and score.
  • Your settings, reminder times and any custom sites you add to the blocker list.

Deleting the app, or using Erase all data in Settings, removes this information from your phone.

2. The AI coach (Pro)

Pro includes a live coach. When you ask it for a message, the app sends a small request to our coach server. It contains:

  • The tone you picked (roast or support).
  • The type of moment (reset, panic or daily).
  • The reason tag you chose, if any.
  • Your current streak and longest streak, in days.
  • Your language (English or Spanish).
  • A random device ID. The app creates it by itself on your phone. It is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or any other account.

No name, email, or free text you type (such as check-in notes) is sent.

Our server passes this information to Anthropic, an AI company, whose model writes the reply. The messages are generated by AI. They can be blunt in roast mode, they are for motivation only, and they may be wrong. Our server does not save the content of your request or the reply. It keeps only the short-lived counters described in section 3. Anthropic handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy. If the coach cannot be reached, or you are not on Pro, the app shows a built-in message that is written on your device instead.

3. Rate limits

To stop abuse, our coach server keeps a counter for each random device ID and each network (IP) address, per day. The counters expire after about a day. The server does not store a history of your requests.

4. Purchases

Pro is bought through Apple in-app purchase. Apple handles all payment, and we never see your card or other payment details. We use RevenueCat, a purchase service, to check whether your Pro access is active. RevenueCat gives your install an anonymous ID, not linked to your name or email, and receives your purchase and subscription status from Apple. RevenueCat's handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy.

5. Usage diagnostics sent with your RevenueCat ID

We do not use any advertising or analytics SDK. We do record a small set of usage events so we can see how people move through the app and the purchase screen. To do this, the app attaches a few simple values to your anonymous RevenueCat ID. They are:

  • How many times the purchase screen was opened, and where it was opened from.
  • When a free trial started, and when the purchase screen was closed without starting one.
  • The time of your last reset. We send only the time, never the reason tag.
  • The time you last used the panic button.
  • The time of your last coach request, and whether the reply came from the live coach or the built-in fallback.
  • The time you completed the starting quiz, and your quiz score.
  • The time of your last check-in. We send the time only, never your mood, urge level or notes.
  • The time you last opened a share screen, and which one.
  • The time you opened the Safari blocker guide, and the time the blocker was first seen switched on.
  • The time you were asked to allow notifications, and your answer.
  • Your install date, and whether your install started with the trial screen first or with the free tier.
  • How many times, and when, the full-screen purchase screen was shown at launch, and whether it was skipped.

These values are stored by RevenueCat against your anonymous ID. We cannot tie them to your name, because we do not have it. Because RevenueCat holds them, the app cannot delete them from inside Settings. To ask for them to be removed, contact us (section 13).

6. Apple Search Ads attribution

The app asks RevenueCat to collect an Apple Search Ads attribution token. If you reached the app from an Apple Search Ads campaign, this lets RevenueCat and us see that the install came from that campaign. It is used only to measure whether our ads work. It does not show you ads inside the app.

7. Things we do not do

  • No account, email or sign in.
  • No ads inside the app and no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or crash tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites. The app does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you in that sense.
  • We do not sell your data.

8. Reminders and notifications

Reminders are local notifications that your phone schedules by itself. We do not run a push notification server. You can turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings.

9. The Safari content blocker

The Safari content blocker runs on your device. Its list of blocked sites is saved on your phone, and Safari applies it by itself. Apple does not share your browsing with the app. We do not see which sites you visit or which ones were blocked.

10. Children

Stop Gooning is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Your choices

  • Use Erase all data in Settings to remove what is stored on your phone.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
  • Ask us about the limited data held on our servers or at RevenueCat by using the contact below. Because we do not know who you are, we may need your anonymous RevenueCat ID to find your records.

12. Not medical advice, and where to get help

Stop Gooning is a self-help tool, not therapy or medical care. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline). Elsewhere, find a local helpline at https://findahelpline.com.
